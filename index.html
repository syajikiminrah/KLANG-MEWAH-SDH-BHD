One Specialty. Complete A/C Care.

Klang Mewah Sdn Bhd provides vehicle air-conditioning inspection, servicing, diagnosis and repair for many types of vehicles.

From weak cooling and refrigerant leaks to compressors, cooling coils, blowers and A/C electrical components, our work is focused specifically on the vehicle air-conditioning system.

A/C PROBLEM?

Air-Cond Not Cold?

If your vehicle's A/C is no longer cooling properly, bring it to Klang Mewah for inspection and diagnosis.

❄️ Not Cold A/C blowing but not cooling.
🌬️ Weak Airflow Airflow feels weaker than usual.
💨 Refrigerant Leak A/C refrigerant may be leaking.
⚙️ Compressor Possible compressor-related A/C problem.
🔊 Unusual Noise Unexpected sound from the A/C system.
👃 Unusual Smell Unpleasant smell when using the A/C.
